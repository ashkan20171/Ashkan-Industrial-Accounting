# Target Feature Matrix

## Financial accounting
General ledger, chart of accounts, journals, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, fiscal periods, closing and recurring entries.

## Industrial accounting
Cost centers, work centers, BOM/routing costing, production/work orders, direct material, direct labor, machine hour rates, overhead pools/drivers, WIP, standard vs actual cost and variance analysis.

## Commercial
Customers, suppliers, quotations, sales/purchase invoices, returns, receivables/payables, aging, credit limits.

## Inventory
Multi-warehouse, lots/serials, reorder points, transfers, cycle counts, valuation, stock card.

## Governance
RBAC, approval workflow, audit trail, period lock, backup/restore, session policy, password policy and change history.

## Internationalization
English default, Persian switch, LTR/RTL, bilingual account names, locale-ready currency/date formatting.
